About Our NSS Unit

The National Service Scheme unit at our college — serving the community through blood donation, social awareness, and volunteerism.

Our Story

Serving the Nation,
One Drop at a Time

The National Service Scheme (NSS) was launched by the Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports, Government of India. Our college unit carries forward this legacy by actively serving the community through voluntary blood donation and civic engagement.

Our blood donation initiative connects student volunteers, faculty, and voluntary donors across departments. Through organized campus drives and emergency coordination, our NSS unit is dedicated to building an accessible, lifesaver network within our college community.

The BloodLink portal is our digital initiative to make this program transparent, efficient, and responsive — connecting verified donors with those in need during medical emergencies.

Our Core

Mission, Vision & Values

🎯

Our Mission

To create a readily available pool of voluntary blood donors within the college community and ensure that no life is lost for lack of blood among our students, staff, and their families.

🔭

Our Vision

To build a self-sustaining blood donation ecosystem where every student is aware of the importance of blood donation and at least 1 in 5 students is a registered donor.

💎

Our Values

Volunteerism, Compassion, Integrity, and Community. We believe that giving blood is one of the most powerful acts of humanity — it costs nothing but can save everything.

Our Journey

Milestones

🌱
Year 1
First Blood Donation Camp
Organized our very first blood donation camp with 20 donors. The response from students was overwhelming and encouraged us to make it a regular initiative.
📈
Year 2
Expanding the Campus Network
Expanded our donor coordination network across college departments to facilitate rapid blood requests for students, faculty, and their families in emergency situations.
💻
Year 3
BloodLink Portal Launch
Launched the BloodLink digital portal to automate donor management, enable online registration, and allow real-time blood group searches within the campus.
🏆
Today
Active Volunteer Network
With 56 dedicated NSS student volunteers and an active digital registry, our unit coordinates emergency blood requests and organizes voluntary donation drives across the campus.
