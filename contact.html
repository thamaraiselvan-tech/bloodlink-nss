Emergency Blood Coordination

The NSS BloodLink Unit acts as a rapid bridge connecting patients, hospitals, and critical blood requirements with verified student donors on campus.

📝
1. Submit Blood Request
Provide patient requirements, blood group, units needed, and hospital location in the form.
🎽
2. NSS Coordinator Dispatch
Your request is logged directly to our live database and alerted to our student volunteer coordinators.
🩸
3. Rapid Campus Donor Match
Our unit checks eligible, active campus donors and facilitates immediate donor support for the patient.
📋 Request Guidelines
  • ✓ Specify the exact hospital name, ward, and contact person.
  • ✓ Indicate if replacement blood or specialized components (Platelets/RBC) are required.
  • ℹ️ Official campus office directory and faculty helpline contacts will be updated shortly.
